# Companion: the algebraic framework (optional reading)

The mechanisms in this repo were not pulled from nowhere — several were motivated by an algebraic framework that treats parts of the transformer forward pass through the lens of a complex-multiplication (CM) elliptic curve over the imaginary quadratic field of discriminant −163, whose ring of integers is a unique factorization domain (class number one). Two ideas from that framework are *load-bearing and validated here*: **Möbius / square-free embedding reconstruction** (store embeddings on square-free indices, reconstruct the rest by Möbius inversion) and **Frobenius-structured quantization** (quantization as a structure-preserving reduction rather than rounding).

**Status, stated honestly.** The broader framework — that the entire forward pass *is* a sequence of curve endomorphisms, that training *is* analytic-rank maximization of the curve's L-function — is a research program, not a proven result. Its central object (an explicit defining equation for the curve) is not yet constructed, so the grand claims are organizing analogies awaiting proof, not theorems about transformers. **None of it is required** to run, understand, or validate anything in this repository; the measured system stands on its gates alone.

This file is a door for the curious. If you came for the receipts, you don't need to walk through it. If you came for the math, the companion papers go deep — and the right posture toward them is the same one this whole project runs on: believe the parts that have a gate, hold the rest as conjecture until they earn one.
