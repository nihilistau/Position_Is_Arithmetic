# Shannon-Prime update — the discrete substrate grows an autonomous librarian (2026-06-20)

**TL;DR.** Shannon-Prime is an attempt to run a real LLM (frozen instruction-tuned Gemma-4-12B) on a *discrete, exact-integer* math substrate instead of floating point — number theory as the container, not as a trick. Two milestones closed recently, and both bend the same way:

1. **The byte-exact forward** (earlier): the entire 12B forward — RMSNorm, softmax, GELU, RoPE, attention — runs as exact-integer arithmetic on a dual-prime CRT-NTT (`q1=1073738753`, `q2=1073732609`, `M≈2^60`). Default-off it is *byte-identical* to the float baseline (PPL 4.6665, run-to-run bit-identical). The point is **auditability**: deterministic, cross-machine-reproducible inference. Not compression.

2. **The autonomous librarian** (this session): a learned head that, given a chat turn, *recalls the right stored memory or refuses if none is relevant* — live on the served 12B. Receipts: **360/361 instance recall + 50/50 foreign-reject, int16-exact** (gate `G-CHAT-B3-WC-DIV2`), deployed and verified on the metal (`G-CHAT-B3-WC-DEPLOY`).

## What we discovered

- **Hand-designed relevance does not survive contact with the open world.** We tried *nine* hand-built signals to pick the relevant memory — verifier prompts, yes/no bridges, Δ-log-likelihood continuations, consensus, cosine-normalized attention, polarity flips. Every single one failed at small scale, dominated by per-episode bias and "super-attractor" memories. Two of the most principled fixes made it *worse*. These are honest negatives, and we keep them.

- **You cannot measure what the model already knows.** The reason recall was unmeasurable: the test facts were *parametric* — the 12B regenerates them from its weights, so deleting the stored copy strands nothing. The corpus was the wall. The fix is a **novel needle** (a secret the model can't know) plus a **teacher-forced ablation knockout**: force the secret's tokens, delete exactly their source memory rows, measure the collapse in log-likelihood. Novel needle collapses **−33.6 nats**; a parametric fact, **−0.15**. A ~220× separation — a clean, shippable relevance gate, and a *perfect labeler* for training.

- **The binding constraint was corpus diversity, not the machinery.** With the labeler, a learned head went from **34% → 100%** instance recall the moment the training corpus used distinct subjects and phrasings instead of shared boilerplate. And a subtle one: the right way to score relevance is *log-sum-exp over positions, then mean over heads* — under that reduction the int16-quantized head is **bit-for-bit identical** to float. The earlier "quantization hurts" result was a wrong-reduction bug, not quantization.

## What it means

The same boundary thesis keeps holding: **the discrete substrate wins on the container (exact arithmetic, determinism, an auditable memory), not on imposing number-theoretic structure on the content.** Every structure-on-content lever we tried — for compression *and* for recall — is a measured negative. The win is the indestructible algebraic container plus a *learned* selector on top.

## The disappointments (stated plainly)

Nine hand-designed recall signals refuted. The "pristine" normalizations hurt. More memory amplified the confounds instead of helping. There is no absolute open-world threshold at small N — the reject lives in a NULL slot of the argmax, not a fixed line.
